# Project 02. County maize

## Question

Which counties produced the most maize in the latest official year,
and what share of the national maize harvest did the top two produce?

## Not the same series

Project 01 cereals, 2024: 4,978,020 tonnes.
Maize is one cereal. A 90 kg bag equals 0.09 tonnes.
Those two figures must not be placed in the same column.

## Licenses read on 16 November 2026

KNBS website footer: "Copyright 2026 © Kenya National Bureau of Statistics | All Rights Reserved."

KilimoSTAT footer: "CC BY 4.0" and "All Rights Reserved" on the same line,
for the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock Development, maintained by KALRO.

## Decision

The county file is not in this repository.
All Rights Reserved does not allow a public copy.
A footer that says both CC BY 4.0 and All Rights Reserved is not a clear grant.
This note records the decision. It does not contain the county table.
